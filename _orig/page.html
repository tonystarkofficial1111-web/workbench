Welcome to Workbench

Your all-in-one workspace for school, coding, engineering, media, AI, arcade games, and more. Pick a tab from above to get started.

Workbench does not associate with anyone and/or anything. Workbench as a whole is a tool, and shall not be treated and/or categorized as a game nor as an AI. Workbench was made with the intention of studying, helping those without resources such as wifi and/or internet access.

DO NOT, under any circumstances SHARE THIS FILE for other abilities, versioning and tracking will be used to enforce such statements.

Need help? New here?

Tutorial

  1. Pick a tab up top — Start, Educational, Media, Arcade, Assistant, User / Saves, or Developer options.
  2. Block World drops you into a 3D voxel world. Use the hotbar to place blocks and tools.
  3. Arcade lists every cabinet. Use the search bar to filter, click ★ to favorite a game, and press Enter to launch the focused cabinet.
  4. Earn coins by playing games — check your balance in the top-right corner.
  5. User / Saves holds your themes, custom accent color, scores, and export/import backups.
  6. Keyboard tips: Arrow keys move between tabs and cabinets; Enter launches; Esc closes a game.

📡 Play with friends?

Connect over your school or home Wi-Fi to share scores and see a live leaderboard with other players on the same network. No internet required — it works on a local network.

⚙ Search settings

Searching works best with your own free YouTube Data API key (Google Cloud Console → enable YouTube Data API v3 → Credentials → Create API key; under “API restrictions” pick YouTube Data API v3 only). Each search or “Load more” costs 100 of the 10,000 free daily units, so about 100 per day. The key stays in this browser only. Without a key, Workbench tries free public search servers, which are often busy or down.

Sync from home server

Download videos from your G16 laptop to your phone over your home Spectrum WiFi. At school, the videos are already on your phone — no cellular data needed for sharing.

How to set up the G16 server:
1. Put your video files (.mp4, .webm, etc.) in your Videos folder on the G16.
2. On the G16, find the g16_video_server.py file and double-click it. A black window opens.
   (If that doesn't work: press the Windows key, type cmd, press Enter. Then type python g16_video_server.py and press Enter.)
3. The black window shows an address like http://192.168.1.42:8787 — copy that down.
4. Leave the black window open. On your phone (same WiFi), open Workbench → Media → Video → Sync.
5. Type the address in the box above and click "Find videos".
6. Tap each video to download it to your phone.
7. At school: open Share, turn on your phone hotspot, and use the code-sharing flow. Videos transfer phone-to-phone over WiFi — no internet needed.

Important: Sync videos while connected to home WiFi. Use the same Workbench file at school that you synced with — videos are stored in this browser's offline storage.

How Video Sharing Works

Videos transfer directly between devices over your hotspot's local network. No internet needed after the initial connection.

Before School — Sync Videos
1
Run the server on your G16
At home, double-click the g16_video_server.py file on your G16. A black window opens and shows an address like http://192.168.1.42:8787. Both your phone and G16 must be on the same Spectrum WiFi. Leave the black window open.
2
Enter the address in Workbench
Open the Videos tab, click "Sync", type in the address from step 1.
3
Download videos
Click "Find videos", then tap each one to download. Videos are saved on your phone. They work offline at school.
At School — Share Videos
1
Turn on hotspot
Turn on your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot. Have your friends connect to it.
2
Open the Share tab
Click "Share". You will see your synced videos with checkboxes. Check which ones to send.
3
Start hosting
Click "Start hosting". A code appears in the text box. Give this code to your friend (type it, show your screen, etc.).
4
Paste their response
Your friend generates a response code. Paste it in the Step 3 box and click "Connect & send".
5
Videos transfer
Videos send one by one. Watch the progress. When done, your friend has the videos in their Library.
Receiver — Getting Videos
1
Connect to hotspot
Make sure you are connected to the host's Wi-Fi hotspot network.
2
Get the host's code
Ask the host for their code. Paste it in the Join section under "Paste the host's code here".
3
Generate response
Click "Generate response". A response code appears. Give this back to the host.
4
Receive videos
Once the host connects, videos transfer automatically. Progress shows on screen. When complete, videos appear in your Library.
5
Watch offline
Go to the Library tab and click "Play" on any video. Works fully offline.
Tips
Compress videos
Use WebM or MP4 format. 720p movies are about 700MB-1.3GB each.
Stay close
The closer devices are to the hotspot, the faster the transfer.
One host at a time
Only one person hosts. Everyone else joins. The host can send to one friend at a time.

Host — Send videos
